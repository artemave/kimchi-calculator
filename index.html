Instructions

  1. Wash & drain. Wash cabbage, drain well, and cut into bite-size pieces. The leaves should be damp, not dripping.
  2. Salt. Mix thoroughly with salt. Rest 45 minutes, turning once halfway through. Do not press.
  3. Rinse & squeeze. Drain brine, rinse once briefly in cold water, then squeeze firmly until no liquid drips.
  4. Season. Mix gochugaru with fish sauce. Add cabbage, spring onions, and optional carrot.
  5. Pack. Pack tightly into a clean jar so cabbage is submerged in its own liquid.
  6. Ferment. Leave at room temperature for 24–48 hours, then refrigerate.

Best eating window

  • Day 1–2: fresh and bright
  • Day 3–5: balanced classic kimchi
  • 1+ week: sharper, ideal for cooking
